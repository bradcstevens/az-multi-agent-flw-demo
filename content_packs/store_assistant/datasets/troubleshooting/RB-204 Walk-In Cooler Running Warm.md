# RB-204 Walk-In Cooler Running Warm

Document ID: RB-204
Site: Brightpath Convenience Store 223
Equipment: Backroom walk-in cooler with four-door reach-in front
Asset tag: BC-223-WIC-01
Owner: Store Equipment Support
Version: 1.1

## Symptom

The walk-in cooler reads above 41 degrees Fahrenheit, or the reach-in doors on the shop side feel
warm and the product inside is not cold.

## Ask First

- What does the wall-mounted cooler display read now, and what did it read at the last check?
- Has a delivery been put away in the last two hours, and how long were the doors held open?
- Is the condenser fan in the backroom running, and can you hear it?
- Is there frost or ice on the coil, the fan guard or the floor at the back of the cooler?

## Safety

- Do not chip ice off a coil, a fan guard or a pipe. Ice comes off with the defrost cycle, not
  with a tool.
- Do not prop the cooler door open to check whether it recovers.
- The forty-hour rule does not exist. Chilled product above 41 degrees Fahrenheit for more than
  four hours is discarded.

## Branches

### Branch A - Warm after a delivery

If the reading rose during or just after a delivery:

1. Close the cooler door and the reach-in doors fully and check the seals are seated.
2. Leave the cooler closed for thirty minutes without opening it.
3. Read the display again. A cooler that has taken a full delivery recovers within about thirty
   minutes.
4. If it has recovered, log the reading and no ticket is needed.
5. If it has not, move to Branch B.

### Branch B - Condenser fan is not running

If the condenser fan in the backroom is silent:

1. Check the backroom breaker for the cooler. If it has tripped, do not reset it more than once.
2. Check nothing is stacked against the condenser. A pallet against the grille starves the airflow
   and the cooler will run warm with everything else working.
3. Clear one metre in front of the condenser and wait fifteen minutes.
4. If the fan is still silent, go to Stop and Escalate. Product moves to the backup reach-in now,
   not after the engineer is booked.

### Branch C - Heavy ice on the coil

If there is heavy ice on the coil or the fan guard:

1. Leave the ice alone.
2. Check the door seal and the door closer. A door that does not pull itself shut is the usual
   cause of a frozen coil.
3. Move product to the backup reach-in and go to Stop and Escalate, reporting the ice and the door
   closer together.

### Branch D - Cooler is cold and only the shop-side doors are warm

If the walk-in is at temperature and the reach-in doors are not:

1. Check the reach-in doors are closing and latching. A pack of cans in the door track holds a
   door a centimetre open all shift.
2. Clear the track, close the door and check the seal pulls in.
3. Pull the front-facing product forward so it is not against the back wall vents.
4. Wait twenty minutes and re-read. If the doors are still warm, go to Stop and Escalate.

## Stop and Escalate

Stop and raise a service ticket when any of these is true:

- The cooler has not recovered thirty minutes after the doors were closed.
- The condenser fan does not run, or the breaker has tripped once already.
- There is heavy ice on the coil or the fan guard.
- Product had to be moved or discarded for a temperature fault.

The ticket needs the asset tag BC-223-WIC-01, the last two logged readings, whether product was
moved or discarded, and the steps already tried.
