# RB-201 Coffee Brewer Not Brewing

Document ID: RB-201
Site: Brightpath Convenience Store 223
Equipment: Twin-head filter coffee brewer, front counter
Asset tag: BC-223-COF-01
Owner: Store Equipment Support
Version: 1.2

## Symptom

An associate presses a brew head and nothing brews, or the cycle starts and stops early, or the
carafe fills with water that is not hot.

## Ask First

- Was the brew head rinsed and a fresh paper filter loaded before the cycle was started?
- Has the machine been switched off at the wall and back on in the last ten minutes?
- Is the water filter indicator green, amber or red?
- Did the other brew head work in the same period?

## Safety

- Do not open the machine housing. Do not remove a panel held by screws.
- Do not pour water into the hopper or the top of the machine to prime it.
- Scald risk: the hot water tank stays hot for around thirty minutes after power off.

## Branches

### Branch A - No lights and no display

If the brew head shows no lights and the display is dark:

1. Check the machine is switched on at the wall socket and the socket switch is up.
2. Check the counter circuit by plugging the till-side kettle into the same socket. If the kettle
   does not heat, the socket is dead: move to Stop and Escalate and report a dead socket, not a
   dead brewer.
3. If the socket is live and the machine is still dark, switch the machine off at the wall, wait
   sixty seconds, and switch it back on.
4. If the display comes up, run one water-only cycle before brewing to coffee.

### Branch B - Display is lit and the cycle will not start

If the display is lit but pressing the head does nothing:

1. Read the display. A "FILL" or "HEATING" message means the tank is refilling, which takes up to
   eight minutes from cold. Wait and try again.
2. Check the water supply tap behind the machine is fully open.
3. Check the brew basket is seated all the way into its rails. A basket a few millimetres proud
   holds the interlock open and the machine will not start.
4. Remove the basket, refit it firmly, and start the cycle again.
5. If it still will not start, move to Branch D.

### Branch C - Brews but the coffee is weak, cold or short

If a cycle runs but the result is wrong:

1. Confirm a fresh paper filter and a full pre-measured pack were used. A reused filter is the
   most common cause of a weak brew.
2. Check the grind setting is on the marked notch on the grinder collar.
3. Check the water filter indicator. Red means the filter is overdue and the brew will run cold
   and slow: fit a new filter cartridge from the backroom stock and run one water-only cycle.
4. Check the spray head for scale. If the spray pattern is broken into a single stream, soak the
   spray head in the descaler for fifteen minutes and refit it.
5. Brew again and taste. If the result is still wrong, move to Stop and Escalate.

### Branch D - Water under or around the machine

If there is water on the counter or the floor under the machine:

1. Switch the machine off at the wall immediately and place the out-of-service tag on it.
2. Close the water supply tap behind the machine.
3. Put a wet-floor sign out and dry the area. A wet counter near a socket is the hazard, not the
   coffee.
4. Do not run another cycle to see if it leaks again.
5. Go straight to Stop and Escalate. A leak is a service call, not a shift fix.

## Stop and Escalate

Stop and raise a service ticket when any of these is true:

- The machine leaks water from anywhere other than the drip tray.
- The display shows an error code beginning with E.
- Branch B and Branch C have both been worked through and the brew is still wrong.
- The machine is dead and the socket is live.

Tag the machine out of service, tell the shift lead, and move the coffee offer to the backup
airpots. The ticket needs the asset tag BC-223-COF-01 and the steps already tried.
