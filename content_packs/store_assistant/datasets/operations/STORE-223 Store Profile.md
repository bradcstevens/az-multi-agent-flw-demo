# STORE-223 Store Profile

Document ID: STORE-223
Site: Circle K Store 223
Owner: Store Operations
Version: 1.0

Everything in this profile is invented. There is no real store, no real employee, no real vendor
and no real telephone number here. It exists so that a ticket raised on this demonstration can be
filled in from a record rather than from the associate's memory.

## Site

- Site name: Circle K Store 223
- Site number: 223
- Format: Forecourt and shop. Eight fuel positions, no vehicle services on site.
- Address: 4180 Halden Road, Marchfield
- Region: Central
- Trading hours: 05:00 to 24:00, seven days
- Backroom access: rear yard, code held by the shift lead
- Delivery window: chilled 06:00 to 08:00, ambient 13:00 to 15:00

## Roles On Shift

- Shift lead: one per shift, holds the safe and the emergency stop reset authorization
- Associates: two on day shift, one on late shift
- Duty manager: covers Store 223 and four neighbouring sites, reachable during trading hours
- The device this assistant runs on is shared. It is not any one associate's device.

## Service Windows

These are the response times the service desk works to for this site. Quote them from here; do not
promise anything faster.

- Priority 1, trade stopped: on site within 4 hours, any trading day
- Priority 2, one asset out of service: on site within 1 working day
- Priority 3, degraded but trading: on site within 3 working days
- Priority 4, cosmetic or scheduled: next planned visit

A food-safety fault is never below priority 2, whatever else is true of it.

## Contacts

- Service desk: raise through the assistant. Every ticket raised here is simulated.
- Duty manager: named on the shift board, trading hours only
- Forecourt support: through the service desk, priority 1 for a whole-forecourt outage
- Out of hours: shift lead calls the duty manager, who decides whether to call it out

## What This Site Does Not Have

Store 223 is a forecourt-and-shop site. It has no vehicle services, no bakery oven, no deli
counter and no pharmacy. A question about any of those has no procedure and no runbook here, and
the honest answer is that it is not covered rather than an improvised one.
