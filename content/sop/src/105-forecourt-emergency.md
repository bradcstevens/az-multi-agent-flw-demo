## Purpose

Give every associate at Brightpath Convenience Store 223 one memorised order of actions for a fuel
emergency on the forecourt, so that the pumps are stopped before anything else is attempted.

## Scope

Applies to every associate on shift, including associates in training. Nothing in this procedure
requires a manager's approval first. When in doubt, press the emergency stop.

## Before You Start

- Know where both emergency stop buttons are: behind the register at knee height and on the
  forecourt column nearest the air pump.
- Know where the two dry-powder extinguishers and the spill kit are stored.
- Never use water on a fuel spill and never use a mobile phone standing over the spill.

## Procedure

1. Press the nearest emergency stop button. This cuts power to every dispenser and to the
   submersible pumps at once.
2. Use the forecourt intercom to tell everyone to stop fueling, switch off their engines and step
   away from the vehicles.
3. Call emergency services if there is any fire, any injury, any spill larger than a dinner plate,
   or any smell of fuel inside the building.
4. Call the duty manager and state the pump number, the fuel grade and your best estimate of the
   volume spilled.
5. Keep everyone at least 25 feet from the spill. Do not start any vehicle inside that ring, do not
   let anyone smoke, and do not switch any electrical equipment on or off in that ring.
6. If the spill is smaller than a dinner plate and no vapor is entering the building, put on the
   gloves and goggles from the spill kit and surround the spill with the absorbent socks.
7. Cover the spill with absorbent granules from the outside edge inward. Never sweep it toward the
   drain.
8. Leave the granules for 10 minutes, then sweep them into the labelled waste drum with the plastic
   scoop from the kit. Never use a metal shovel.
9. Seal the drum, label it with the date, the fuel grade and your initials, and move it to the
   marked outdoor storage area.
10. Photograph the area, then complete the forecourt incident record with the time of the stop, the
    time of each call and the volume recovered.
11. Do not reset the emergency stop yourself. Only the duty manager or a district manager restores
    dispenser power, after confirming the area is clear.
12. Replace every item used from the spill kit before the end of the shift and note the restock on
    the shift handover sheet.

## If Something Is Wrong

- Fire at a dispenser: press the emergency stop, call emergency services, evacuate the forecourt
  and the shop, and use an extinguisher only if the fire is smaller than a waste bin and you have
  a clear path away from it.
- Fuel on a person's skin or clothing: move them away from the forecourt, flush the skin with water
  for 15 minutes, remove soaked clothing, and call emergency services.
- Vehicle drives off with the nozzle still attached: press the emergency stop, do not chase the
  vehicle, record the plate if it is safe to do so, and call the duty manager.

## Escalation

Emergency services first whenever there is fire, injury or vapor indoors. Duty manager in every
other case, and the district safety line within one hour for any spill that used the kit.
