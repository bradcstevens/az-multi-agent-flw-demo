## Purpose

Hand a shift over at Circle K Store 223 in a fixed 10 minutes so that nothing
in-flight is lost between two associates, and so the incoming associate starts knowing what is
already broken, already counted and already promised to a customer.

## Scope

Applies to the outgoing and incoming associates at every shift change, and to the shift lead who
reviews the sheet at the end of the day.

## Before You Start

- The handover happens at the back counter, not at the register, and both associates are present.
- The outgoing associate completes their final drop before the handover starts.
- A handover is never done by text message or by leaving a note on the counter.

## Procedure

1. Print the shift-end report and bring it to the back counter with the shift handover sheet.
2. Walk the incoming associate through the four standing lines on the sheet: cash, food safety,
   equipment and customer promises.
3. Under cash, state the number of drops made, the closing drawer total and any variance recorded
   under SOP-106.
4. Under food safety, state the last temperature reading time, any out-of-range reading and any
   discard made under SOP-107.
5. Under equipment, list every item currently out of service, when it was tagged and who was told.
6. Under customer promises, list every held item, special order, rain check and callback the store
   has promised, with the customer's first name only and the time promised.
7. Note the current state of the restroom log and any inspection slot that was missed under
   SOP-103.
8. Note any incident from the shift: a refusal under SOP-108, a spill under SOP-105, a delivery
   shortage under SOP-109.
9. Read the task board together and move any task that was not completed into the incoming shift's
   column, with a reason. Never delete an uncompleted task.
10. Both associates sign and time the sheet. The incoming associate's signature means they were
    told, not that they agree.
11. File the sheet in the handover folder and post the top three carry-over tasks on the tablet
    task board so the district sees them.
12. The outgoing associate stays until the sheet is signed, then logs out of the terminal.

## What Always Goes on the Sheet

- Any equipment out of service, with the tag time.
- Any cash variance over five dollars.
- Any food discard and the reason.
- Any promise made to a customer.
- Any missed restroom inspection slot.
- Any task carried over, and why.

## If Something Is Wrong

- The incoming associate does not arrive: do not leave. Call the shift lead and stay until relieved.
- The sheet from the previous shift is missing: start a new sheet, write "previous sheet missing"
  on the first line and tell the shift lead.
- The two associates disagree about a count: record both numbers on the sheet and call the shift
  lead for a witnessed recount rather than settling it between yourselves.

## Escalation

Shift lead for any unrelieved shift, any disputed count and any carry-over task that has moved
across three shifts without being completed.
