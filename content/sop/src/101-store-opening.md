## Purpose

Bring the store from overnight lockup to full trading condition safely, in a fixed order, so that
the first customer of the day is served by a store that is lit, stocked, priced and secure.

## Scope

Applies to the opening associate and the shift lead at Brightpath Convenience Store 223. The
opening routine starts 45 minutes before the posted opening time.

## Before You Start

- Carry your own store key fob and the alarm code issued to you. Never use another associate's code.
- Do not unlock the customer doors until step 12.
- If anything looks disturbed outside the building, stop and call the duty manager before entering.

## Procedure

1. Walk the exterior of the building before unlocking anything. Look for broken glass, forced door
   frames, spilled fuel or anyone waiting in the lot.
2. Unlock the staff door only, enter, and disarm the alarm within 45 seconds using your own code.
3. Re-lock the staff door behind you. The store stays locked until the sales floor is ready.
4. Switch on the sales-floor lighting, the forecourt canopy lighting and the exterior sign.
5. Check the overnight alarm log on the panel. Record any fault code on the shift handover sheet
   and report it to the duty manager.
6. Read and record the hot food case and the walk-in cooler temperatures on the daily temperature
   log. Follow SOP-107 if any reading is out of range.
7. Start the coffee bar setup described in SOP-104 so that brewing finishes before opening.
8. Count the opening float in the back office with the safe door closed. Confirm it matches the
   posted float amount and sign the count sheet.
9. Load the register drawer, log in to the point-of-sale terminal and print the shift-start report.
10. Enable the fuel dispensers from the console and confirm every pump shows an idle screen.
11. Walk the sales floor. Front-face the top four aisles, pull anything past its date, and confirm
    the restrooms have been cleaned per SOP-103.
12. Unlock the customer doors at the posted opening time, switch the door sign to OPEN, and turn on
    the outdoor speaker announcement.
13. Send the opening confirmation on the store tablet task board so the district sees the store
    opened on time.

## If Something Is Wrong

- Alarm sounding, door forced, or anyone inside: leave the building, stand in the lot and call the
  duty manager, then emergency services if there is any doubt.
- Power out: keep the doors locked, do not enable the dispensers, and call the duty manager.
- Short float: stop, do not open the drawer for trade, and call the shift lead for a witnessed
  recount.

## Escalation

Duty manager first. If the duty manager cannot be reached within 10 minutes, call the Northgate
District line and record the call on the shift handover sheet.
