## Purpose

Keep the cash on hand at Brightpath Convenience Store 223 low, counted and traceable, so that no
associate is ever standing behind a drawer holding more money than the store can account for.

## Scope

Applies to every associate who operates a register, and to the shift lead who witnesses counts.
The drawer limit is 150 dollars in cash at any time.

## Before You Start

- Only one associate operates a drawer during a shift. Never share a login or a drawer.
- Count cash out of customer view, in the back office, with the door closed.
- The safe is a delayed-drop safe. Nobody in the store can open the deposit chamber.

## Procedure

1. At the start of your shift, count the opening float with the shift lead present and both of you
   sign the count sheet.
2. Log in to the point-of-sale terminal with your own credentials and confirm the drawer number on
   the screen matches the drawer you were handed.
3. Watch the running cash total on the terminal during the shift. When it reaches 150 dollars,
   start a drop immediately.
4. To make a drop, select CASH DROP on the terminal, count the notes out of the drawer, and leave
   20 dollars of small notes and the coin for change.
5. Seal the notes in a numbered drop envelope, write the amount, the time, the drawer number and
   your initials on the envelope, and post it into the safe chute.
6. Enter the envelope number and the amount on the terminal and print the drop slip. Staple the
   slip into the drop log.
7. Repeat the drop every time the drawer reaches the limit, and always before you leave the
   register for a break.
8. At the end of your shift, run the shift-end report, count the drawer in the back office, and
   record the counted total, the expected total and the difference on the count sheet.
9. Have the shift lead witness and sign any variance greater than five dollars, and note the
   suspected cause on the same line.
10. Complete the final drop of the shift, return the float to the posted amount, and hand the
    drawer to the shift lead.
11. Record the number of drops made and any variance on the shift handover sheet.

## Never Do This

- Never count cash on the sales floor or in view of the forecourt cameras' blind corner.
- Never move money between two drawers to make a count balance.
- Never accept a note larger than 50 dollars, and never accept a large note without the counterfeit
  pen check.
- Never tell a customer how much cash is in the drawer or when the deposit is collected.

## If Something Is Wrong

- Robbery: comply, do not resist, do not follow anyone out of the building, press the silent alarm
  only when it is safe, then lock the doors and call emergency services.
- Suspected counterfeit note: do not return the note to the customer if it is safe to keep it,
  note the description of the customer after they leave, and call the duty manager.
- Drawer short by more than ten dollars: stop trading on that drawer, leave the count as it stands,
  and call the shift lead for a witnessed recount.

## Escalation

Shift lead for any variance over five dollars. Duty manager for any variance over 25 dollars, any
suspected counterfeit note and any robbery, and the loss-prevention line within 24 hours.
